Jongdosanim Ahn Gyeong-jeon
Jongdosanim of Jeung San Do

Jongdosanim is the supreme spiritual leader and highest teacher of Jeung San Do, carrying forward the authentic lineage of Jeungsan Sangjenim, the founder of Jeung San Do, and Taemonim, the Great Mother. He represents Jeung San Do both within Korea and throughout the world.

Following the ascension of the former Jongdosanim, Ahn Un-san, the present supreme leader of Jeung San Do is Ahn Gyeong-jeon Jongdosanim. The late Ahn Un-san Jongdosanim is revered as the Taesang Jongdosanim 太上宗道師, who opened the Third Transformation of the Harvest Work of the Dao following Korea's liberation on August 15, 1945. He is honored as both the father of the Truth and the father of spiritual practice.

Since the 1970s, Ahn Gyeong-jeon Jongdosanim has assisted Taesang Jongdosanim Ahn Un-san and helped lead Jeung San Do. Over the decades, he has published numerous influential works including The Truth of Jeung San Do, This Is Gaebyeok, and The Actual Situation of Gaebyeok. Through these publications, he has conveyed profound spiritual insight and a powerful message concerning the transformation of human civilization.

Above all, through rigorous field research and historical verification, he compiled and published the Dojeon 道典, the sacred scripture of Jeung San Do. Through this monumental work, he organized and transmitted the world of Sangjenim's Great Dao and opened humanity's eyes to the true vision of the Truth.

Since 1982, he has continuously edited and published annotated editions of Hwandan Gogi 桓檀古記. The annotated edition published in 2012 represents the culmination of thirty years of scholarship and dedication.

Through the Autumn Gaebyeok, the entire civilizational order of the world is being fundamentally transformed, and a new world will emerge. Jeung San Do is the truth of salvation that seeks to save oneself, one's family, one's neighbors, and humanity during the time of Gaebyeok. Jongdosanim continues to cultivate leaders and spiritual pioneers who will help save humanity and open a new era.
